Frequently asked questions

What types of properties do you clean?

We work with offices, retail spaces, professional suites, the office areas of warehouses and other commercial properties. If yours is different, tell us and we will let you know if we can help.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form or call us. We confirm the scope with you and send a written quote within 1 business day.

Do you offer one-time and recurring cleaning?

Both. Recurring plans are set by frequency and one-time services by scope, and both are detailed in your quote.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. We communicate in English and Spanish, and this website is available in both.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve the Midwest states listed in our service areas. Contact us if you are unsure whether your location is covered.
