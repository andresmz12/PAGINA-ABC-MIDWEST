Day Porter Service

A day porter looks after high-traffic areas while the building is in use, handling the small things that cannot wait for the evening clean.

Coverage hours and tasks are tailored to your site and written into the quote, from a few hours a day to a full schedule.

Get a quote

What is included

The exact scope is confirmed in your written quote. Typical tasks include:

Restroom checks

Regular checks, wipe-downs and restocking of supplies during the day.

Lobby and entrance upkeep

Keeping reception areas, entry glass and walkways presentable.

Breakroom and common areas

Tidying shared kitchens, meeting rooms and seating areas between uses.

Spills and quick response

On-site attention to spills, trash and similar needs as they come up.

Questions about this service

How many hours of coverage can we choose?

Coverage is defined by your needs, from a few hours to a full day. We set it in the quote.

Is a day porter the same as evening cleaning?

No. Day porter service maintains the space during business hours and complements, rather than replaces, a regular cleaning plan.

Can the same team handle both services?

We can propose both services together so they are planned as one routine.
