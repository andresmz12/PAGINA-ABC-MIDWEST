Floor Care

Floors take the most wear in any building and are the first thing visitors notice. Floor care goes beyond daily mopping, using equipment and methods suited to each surface.

We assess the flooring type and its condition, then recommend a plan, one-time or recurring, and write it into your quote.

Get a quote

What is included

The exact scope is confirmed in your written quote. Typical tasks include:

Machine scrubbing

Deep cleaning of hard floors to lift embedded dirt that routine mopping leaves behind.

Buffing and finish care

Buffing and, where suitable for the floor, finish application to protect and refresh the surface.

Carpet maintenance

Vacuuming and extraction cleaning for carpeted areas, with spot treatment on request.

Maintenance schedule

A periodic plan so floor care happens before wear becomes visible.

Questions about this service

What types of flooring do you work with?

Common commercial surfaces such as vinyl, tile, sealed concrete and carpet. Tell us what you have and we will confirm the right method.

Will the area be closed during floor care?

Some treatments need dry time. We plan the work for low-traffic periods and tell you in advance which areas need to stay clear.

Is floor care a one-time or recurring service?

Either. Many sites combine routine cleaning with periodic deep floor care.
