Post-Construction Cleaning

After a build-out or renovation, fine dust settles on every surface. Post-construction cleaning removes debris and residue so the space can be inspected, furnished and opened.

The scope depends on the size and condition of the project. We review the details with you and put the stages, access needs and price in writing.

Get a quote

What is included

The exact scope is confirmed in your written quote. Typical tasks include:

Debris and dust removal

Removal of loose debris and fine dust from floors, ledges, vents accessible without tools and built-in surfaces.

Surfaces and fixtures

Cleaning of counters, cabinets, doors, trim and installed fixtures, including sticker and residue removal where safe.

Glass and frames

Interior glass, mirrors and window frames cleaned and checked for streaks.

Final detail pass

A last walk-through pass to touch up details before hand-over.

Questions about this service

When should post-construction cleaning be scheduled?

Usually after the contractor finishes the main work and before furniture or operations move in. We coordinate timing with your project schedule.

Do you handle construction waste removal?

Bulk construction waste and hazardous materials are normally handled by the contractor. We will confirm what is and is not covered in your quote.

Can the cleaning be done in stages?

Yes. Rough, final and touch-up cleans can be planned as separate visits if the project needs it.
